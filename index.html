The Peony

My favourite flower

The peony is my favourite flower because it manages to be soft and dramatic at the same time. A single bud starts out as a tight green ball, no bigger than a marble, and then over a few warm days it opens into a huge ruffled bloom packed withdozen of petals. The colours run from snow white through blush pink to deep wine red, and some varieties are a pale butter yellow.

Peonies are grown all over the world, but they are especially loved on China, where they have been planted in gardens for more than a thousand years and are often called the king of flowers. They are also a favourite in wedding bouquets. What I like most is how long lived they are: a peony plant can keep flowering in the same spot for fifty years or more, outliving the gardener who planted it. They need very little fussing, just sunshine, good soil and a bit of patince in the first season.

Why I like peonies

  • Hug, ruffled blooms
  • A sweet, light perfume
  • Many colours to choose from
  • THey come back every year
  • They last for years in one garden
